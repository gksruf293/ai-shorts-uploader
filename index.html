AI Shorts Uploader

Internal publishing tool · operated by the channel owner

This tool uses YouTube API Services. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

AI Shorts Uploader is a private command-line tool that the owner of six educational YouTube channels uses to upload their own short videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Uploads finished short videos (produced locally) to the owner's own YouTube channels with the videos.insert endpoint.
  • Sets the title, description, tags, language and privacy status that the owner prepared for each video.
  • Uploads a custom thumbnail with thumbnails.set and posts the owner's pinned-comment text with commentThreads.insert.
